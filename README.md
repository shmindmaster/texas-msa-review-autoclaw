# Texas MSA review package, built with AutoClaw

**Built with AutoClaw, Round 3, Legal Practice track.** Everything here is fictional: the parties, the contract and the numbers. None of it is legal advice.

## The problem

Small Texas companies sign vendor contracts without a lawyer in the room, and many of the traps are specific to Texas: non-competes under Bus. & Com. Code § 15.50, indemnity for your own negligence, out-of-state venue, and a renewal window buried in the boilerplate.

## What I gave AutoClaw

A brief ([`00-brief.md`](00-brief.md)) asking for a careful in-house review for a non-lawyer operations lead. It listed seven problems to plant in a customer-favorable services agreement, and it required a Texas primary source for every flag, no outcome predictions, and escalation of anything uncertain to a licensed Texas attorney.

## What I checked myself

All 35 citations were then checked against the primary sources: the Texas statute text and the full opinions ([`08-citation-check.md`](08-citation-check.md)).

* Every statute AutoClaw cited is real.
* Three case citations were wrong. One cited *Marsh USA v. Cook* for the opposite of what it holds, one gave the wrong reporter citation for *Sheshunoff*, and one cited *Reyes* for a rule that case doesn't state.
* 17 more needed a correction or caveat, such as the 2025 amendment to § 15.52 or the TRAIGA chapter numbering.

The `-v2` files apply every fix. The first-draft audit is kept so you can see what changed.

## What to reuse

* Plant known problems in a fixture contract, then measure whether the review finds all of them.
* Redline only what the playbook flags, so every change traces to a rule and a citation.
* Treat an AI's own citation audit as a first pass, then check every citation against the statute text and the opinion itself.
