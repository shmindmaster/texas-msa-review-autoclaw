# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

> **FICTIONAL PRODUCT-DEMO FIXTURE.** This agreement is a synthetic test document for a Texas commercial-contract review tool. No real party, matter, or transaction is involved. It is not legal advice and must not be used as a form.

This Master Services Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of November 1, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between:

**Bluebonnet Field Services LLC**, a Texas limited liability company with its principal place of business at 4100 Congress Avenue, Suite 220, Austin, Texas 78701 ("**Provider**"); and

**Pecan Creek Logistics, Inc.**, a Texas corporation with its principal place of business at 1200 Smith Street, Suite 900, Houston, Texas 77002 ("**Customer**").

Provider and Customer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Provider furnishes equipment maintenance technicians and related field services to commercial customers in Texas.

B. Customer owns and operates warehousing and distribution facilities and desires to engage Provider to maintain material handling equipment at certain of those facilities.

C. The Parties wish to set out the terms on which Provider will provide the services described in this Agreement.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "**Service Data**" means all data, records, logs, telemetry, photographs, work orders, technician notes, and other information collected, generated, or processed by Provider or its Personnel in connection with the Services, including equipment maintenance histories and performance metrics.

1.2 "**Personnel**" means the employees, contractors, and subcontractors of Provider who perform the Services.

1.3 "**Services**" means the equipment maintenance services described in Section 2 and in each Statement of Work.

1.4 "**Statement of Work**" or "**SOW**" means a statement of work executed by the Parties that identifies the facilities, equipment, and scope of Services.

1.5 "**Monthly Fee**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

## 2. SERVICES; SCOPE

2.1 **Provision of Services.** Provider shall furnish trained equipment maintenance technicians to perform preventive maintenance, corrective repairs, and related field services on Customer's material handling equipment at the facilities identified in each Statement of Work.

2.2 **Statements of Work.** Each Statement of Work is governed by this Agreement. In the event of a conflict, this Agreement controls unless the Statement of Work expressly states that it amends a numbered section of this Agreement.

2.3 **Standard of Performance.** Provider shall perform the Services in a good and workmanlike manner consistent with generally accepted industry standards for equipment maintenance services of this type, and shall supply tools, test equipment, and replacement parts (other than parts stocked by Customer) as required for the Services.

2.4 **Personnel.** Provider shall assign Personnel who are trained and qualified for the work. Provider remains solely responsible for all wages, benefits, tax withholding, and workers' compensation coverage for its Personnel, and nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, joint-employment, or co-employment relationship between Customer and any Personnel.

2.5 **Site Rules.** Provider and its Personnel shall comply with Customer's reasonable site safety and security rules as provided in writing to Provider, while performing Services at Customer's facilities.

## 3. TERM

3.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for twenty-four (24) months (the "**Initial Term**"), unless earlier terminated under Section 13.

3.2 **Renewal.** The term and renewal of this Agreement are governed exclusively by Section 22.6, and the Parties agree that Section 22.6 is the sole provision addressing renewal or extension of the term.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 **Fees.** Customer shall pay Provider a monthly fee of Eighteen Thousand Dollars ($18,000.00) (the "**Monthly Fee**"), invoiced monthly in advance. The Monthly Fee is fixed for the Initial Term.

4.2 **Payment Terms.** Amounts due under this Agreement are due and payable ninety (90) days after Customer's receipt of a correct and complete invoice (**Net 90**). Provider shall not charge interest, late charges, or collection costs on any amount not paid when due.

4.3 **Withholding; Set-Off.** Customer may withhold payment of any amount, for any reason or for no reason, in Customer's sole and absolute discretion, and may set off against any invoice any amount that Customer claims, in its sole discretion, to be owed by Provider, without prior notice to Provider and without any obligation to state the basis for the withholding. Customer's exercise of its rights under this Section 4.3 is not a breach of this Agreement.

4.4 **Taxes.** Fees are exclusive of applicable sales and use taxes, which Customer shall pay.

4.5 **No Other Charges.** Provider shall not invoice travel time, mileage, or administrative surcharges except as expressly agreed in a Statement of Work.

## 5. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR

Provider is and remains an independent contractor. Provider has no authority to bind Customer, and neither Provider nor its Personnel is entitled to participate in any benefit plan of Customer.

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 **Customer Confidential Information.** "**Customer Confidential Information**" means non-public information that Customer discloses to Provider in connection with this Agreement, including facility layouts, equipment inventories, and operating schedules.

6.2 **Exclusions.** Customer Confidential Information does not include information that is or becomes public through no breach of Provider, that Provider can show was already in its possession without obligation of confidentiality, that is independently developed without use of Customer Confidential Information, or that is received from a third party without restriction.

6.3 **Obligations.** Provider shall use Customer Confidential Information solely to perform the Services and shall protect it using at least reasonable care. Provider's obligations under this Section 6 continue for three (3) years after termination.

6.4 **Compelled Disclosure.** Provider may disclose Customer Confidential Information if required by law, provided it gives Customer prompt notice where legally permitted.

## 7. SERVICE DATA; ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

7.1 **Ownership.** As between the Parties, all Service Data is and shall be the sole and exclusive property of Customer. Provider assigns to Customer all right, title, and interest in Service Data.

7.2 **License to Provider.** Customer grants Provider a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use Service Data solely to perform the Services during the term of this Agreement.

7.3 **Artificial Intelligence.** Customer may, at its sole discretion and without restriction of any kind, use Service Data, and permit its affiliates, successors, and vendors to use Service Data, to train, develop, validate, fine-tune, and improve artificial intelligence and machine learning models and systems, including commercial and proprietary models, without any obligation to disclose such use to Provider, to obtain Provider's consent, to pay any additional fee, to anonymize or de-identify Service Data, or to limit such use in any respect. Provider waives any claim, objection, or restriction arising out of such use, including any claim based on trade secret, confidentiality, or data protection grounds.

## 8. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

8.1 **Provider Materials.** Provider retains all right, title, and interest in its pre-existing methodologies, tools, software, and documentation ("**Provider Materials**"). Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free license to use Provider Materials solely as embodied in the deliverables.

8.2 **Deliverables.** All work product, reports, and records created by Provider for Customer under a Statement of Work are works made for hire for Customer, and Provider assigns them to Customer.

## 9. INDEMNIFICATION

9.1 **Provider Indemnity.** Provider shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Customer and its officers, directors, employees, agents, affiliates, and subcontractors from and against any and all claims, demands, actions, causes of action, damages, losses, liabilities, costs, and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of or relating to this Agreement, the Services, or the acts or omissions of Provider or its Personnel, including without limitation claims for bodily injury, death, or property damage, and including without limitation any such claims to the extent caused by or arising out of the negligence, gross negligence, or other fault of Customer or its officers, directors, employees, or agents. Provider's obligations under this Section 9.1 are not limited by any insurance requirement in this Agreement.

9.2 **Customer Indemnity.** Customer shall indemnify Provider against third-party claims for bodily injury or tangible property damage to the extent caused by the gross negligence or willful misconduct of Customer.

9.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified party shall give prompt notice of a claim, and the indemnifying party shall control the defense with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party. The indemnified party may participate at its own expense.

## 10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

10.1 **Provider's Liability.** Provider's aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement, whether in contract, tort, strict liability, or otherwise, is unlimited, and nothing in this Section 10 limits, reduces, or excludes Provider's liability for any claim, damage, or loss.

10.2 **Customer's Liability.** Customer's total aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement, whether in contract, tort, strict liability, or otherwise, shall not exceed the Monthly Fee paid or payable in the one (1) month preceding the event giving rise to the claim (currently $18,000.00), regardless of the form of action, and this cap applies to all claims including claims for indemnity, gross negligence, and willful misconduct, and excludes only Customer's obligation to pay undisputed fees.

10.3 **Consequential Damages.** Customer shall not be liable for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential, punitive, or exemplary damages, or for lost profits or lost business, however caused and on any theory of liability. This Section 10.3 does not apply to Provider, whose liability for such damages remains unlimited.

## 11. NON-COMPETITION

11.1 **Covenant.** During the term of this Agreement and for a period of three (3) years following the expiration or termination of this Agreement for any reason, neither Provider nor any of its Personnel shall, directly or indirectly, as an owner, partner, shareholder, employee, consultant, contractor, investor, or otherwise, provide services to, or engage in the business of, any logistics company, warehousing company, distribution company, or third-party maintenance provider serving any such company, anywhere in the State of Texas.

11.2 **No Additional Consideration.** Provider acknowledges and agrees that the covenants in this Section 11 are given without any consideration beyond the fees payable under this Agreement and are not tied to the disclosure or receipt of any confidential information, trade secret, or customer relationship.

11.3 **Reasonableness; Enforcement.** The Parties agree that the restrictions in this Section 11 are reasonable in time, geography, and scope. Provider agrees that any breach would cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages are inadequate, and that Customer is entitled to injunctive relief without bond, without limiting any other remedy.

## 12. INSURANCE

Provider shall maintain, at its own expense: (a) commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 aggregate; (b) workers' compensation as required by law; (c) commercial automobile liability; and (d) professional liability of not less than $1,000,000. Upon request, Provider shall furnish certificates of insurance. Provider's insurance does not limit its liability under Section 10.1.

## 13. TERMINATION

13.1 **Termination for Convenience by Customer.** Customer may terminate this Agreement or any Statement of Work for convenience on thirty (30) days' prior written notice.

13.2 **Termination for Cause by Customer.** Customer may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if Provider fails to perform and does not cure within ten (10) days after notice, or immediately upon any material breach of Sections 6, 7, 9, or 11.

13.3 **Termination by Provider.** Provider may terminate this Agreement only if Customer fails to pay an undisputed amount and the failure continues for ninety (90) days after written notice.

13.4 **Effect of Termination.** Upon termination, Customer shall pay fees properly due for Services performed through the effective date of termination, subject to Section 4.3. Sections 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 18, and 22 survive.

## 14. RECORDS AND AUDIT

Provider shall maintain records of the Services for three (3) years and shall make them available to Customer on reasonable notice.

## 15. PUBLICITY

Provider shall not use Customer's name or marks in any press release, case study, or marketing material without Customer's prior written consent.

## 16. COMPLIANCE WITH LAWS

Each Party shall comply with applicable law in the performance of this Agreement, including applicable employment, safety, and data protection laws.

## 17. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

17.1 **Negotiation.** The Parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute by good-faith negotiation between their designated representatives for thirty (30) days after written notice of the dispute.

17.2 **Litigation.** If the dispute is not resolved, either Party may pursue its remedies in the courts specified in Section 18.2. Each Party waives any right to a jury trial to the extent permitted by law.

## 18. GOVERNING LAW; VENUE

18.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflicts of law principles.

18.2 **Exclusive Venue.** The exclusive venue for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement is the state and federal courts located in New York County, New York, and each Party irrevocably consents to the personal jurisdiction of those courts and waives any objection based on venue or *forum non conveniens*, including any claim that New York is an inconvenient forum.

## 19. NOTICES

All notices under this Agreement must be in writing and are deemed given when delivered by hand, one (1) business day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or on the date of transmission by email with confirmation of receipt, addressed to the addresses in the preamble (or to such other address as a Party designates).

## 22. GENERAL PROVISIONS

22.1 **Assignment.** Provider may not assign this Agreement without Customer's prior written consent. Customer may assign this Agreement to an affiliate or in connection with a merger, sale of assets, or change of control without consent.

22.2 **Waiver.** No waiver is effective unless in writing. No failure or delay in exercising a right operates as a waiver.

22.3 **Severability.** If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, the remainder continues in effect, and the Parties shall substitute a valid provision that most nearly effects the Parties' intent.

22.4 **Amendment.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. Customer may update site rules and equipment lists by written notice.

22.5 **Notices of Default.** Any notice of default must describe the default in reasonable detail and be sent to the addresses in Section 19.

22.6 **Renewal.** Upon expiration of the Initial Term, this Agreement automatically renews for successive two (2) year terms unless either Party gives the other Party written notice of non-renewal at least one hundred twenty (120) days before the end of the then-current term, in which case this Agreement expires at the end of that term. Notices of non-renewal must be sent to the addresses in Section 19 and are effective only if actually received. There is no obligation on either Party to remind the other of the renewal date or of the notice deadline. 22.7 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including electronic signatures, each of which is deemed an original.

22.8 **Construction.** This Agreement is the product of negotiation between sophisticated commercial parties, each represented by counsel, and no rule of construction against the drafter applies.
