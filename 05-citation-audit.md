# Citation Audit — Texas MSA Review Package

**What this is.** Step 5 of the brief. Every citation appearing in `02-texas-baseline-playbook.md`, `03-redline.md`, and `04-risk-memo.md` is listed below with where it is used, a confidence rating, and notes. Anything that could not be confirmed against a primary or reliable secondary source in this session is marked `[VERIFY]`.

**How citations were checked.** No statute or case-retrieval database was available in this session. Each citation was checked against public web sources, including the Texas Legislature's statute pages (statutes.capitol.texas.gov), the Texas Attorney General's site, court-opinion aggregators, and law-firm commentary. "High" confidence means the citation was confirmed by at least one independent source in this session. "Medium" means the authority is well known but its exact reporter citation or section number was not directly confirmed in this session. "Low" means the point is an inference or an unconfirmed negative, and it is marked `[VERIFY]`.
